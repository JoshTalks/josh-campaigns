  1. Create a WhatsApp Business account on Facebook Business

  • META_WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN - Your WhatsApp Business API access token
  • META_WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_ACCOUNT_ID - Your business account ID
  • META_WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID - Your phone number ID
